# Privacy Policy

**Purnote** is built so that your notes stay yours. This policy explains exactly
what the app does and does not do with your data. It is short on purpose,
because Purnote collects very little.

_Last updated: 22 August 2026._

## The short version

Purnote does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Your notes
are plain Markdown files that live in your own storage.

## Where your notes are stored

Every note you create is saved as an ordinary `.md` file:

- in your **iCloud Drive** (in a folder named `purnote`), if you use iCloud, or
- **on your device only**, if you choose to use Purnote without iCloud.

When iCloud is used, syncing between your devices happens through **your own
Apple iCloud account**, using Apple's infrastructure. Purnote has no server in
between and never receives a copy of your notes. Apple's handling of iCloud data
is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Purnote does not:

- require an account or ask for any personal information;
- include analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind;
- show advertising or use an advertising identifier;
- contact any server operated by the developer — there are none.

Because Purnote collects no data, there is nothing for us to store, share, or
sell, and none of your information ever reaches the developer.

## Third-party code

Purnote uses one open-source library,
[MarkdownUI](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/swift-markdown-ui), solely to
display your notes on your device. It does not collect data or communicate over
the network.

## Your control over your data

Because your notes are plain files in your own iCloud Drive or on your device,
you are always in full control:

- Open, edit, copy, or back them up with any app you like.
- Delete a note in Purnote, or delete the whole `purnote` folder in the Files
  app to remove everything at once.
- If you ever stop using Purnote, your notes remain — nothing is locked in.

## Children's privacy

Purnote collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a
new "last updated" date.
